Plan notes & capacity

The building’s size and floor layout follow the venue’s official drawings. It is 24.38 m wide and 17.07 m deep, with floor heights of 2.89 m on floors 1–3 and 3.04 m on the 4th. The 10 × 5 m LED screen on the east wall and the bar counters are existing fixtures. Each interior drawing cuts its floor 1.2 m above the slab.

The venue guide describes the ground floor as holding about 500 people, and the Seoul Convention Bureau lists ground-floor capacities of 400 in theatre seating, 300 in classroom seating and 200 for banquets. The planned 500 guests (600 with a reserve) combine the 112 chairs, standing room and the 2nd-floor viewing areas, and the number allowed in at one time requires venue approval. The 112 audience chairs, eight competitor desks, stage, lighting truss, six booths, VIP lounge and commentary desk are a proposed layout and still require venue approval, including final clearances and catering access.

The venue hire is 48 hours, assumed to run from 00:00 on 19 December to 00:00 on 21 December. The 16:00–20:00 running order on the 19th follows the event proposal. Venue terms

From the SJ Kunsthalle venue team · received 20 November 2026

Hire period

  • Every hire contract, half-day hire included, counts venue setup and teardown inside the hired hours.

Included free of charge

  • LED glass: two monochrome faces inside (simple text) and one colour face outside (video and images). The venue emails the content guide after the contract.
  • House sound (Bose L1 Pro 32) and four wireless microphones
  • Café tables and chairs for about 100 seats
  • House stage, 6 m wide × 2 m deep, installed on request

Paid options

  • LED screen (10 m wide × 5 m high)
  • Stage LED lighting (moving and colour)

Parking

  • Three free parking spaces come with the hire. Ask the venue about extra parking and details.

Installation and restoration

  • No fixing into the building with screws. Any damage must be restored.
  • After the event the venue must be returned to its original condition, including removing installations and cleaning.

Waste and cleaning

  • Buy standard pay-as-you-throw bags separately and leave them at the designated spot.
  • Recycling goes through the contractor designated by Gangnam-gu, using its bags bought separately.
  • Cleaning is done by a contractor of your choice or by the host team, then checked by the venue.

What this means for the plan

  • The stage in the drawings is 3.55 × 10.70 m, larger than the 6 × 2 m house stage, so the rest must be rented.
  • About 100 of the 112 audience chairs can be the house chairs, leaving 12 to rent.
  • The banner and lighting truss must go up without screws. The truss is ground-supported, and the banner fixing method needs agreeing with the venue.
  • Setup and teardown sit inside the 48-hour hire: the grey rows in the event schedule.
